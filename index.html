06 Services & expertise

Design that works.
Development that delivers.

David Wix is a web developer and graphic designer creating digital experiences that help businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, and brands build a stronger online presence. From professional business websites and high-converting ecommerce stores to visual identities and marketing graphics, each project is shaped to communicate clearly, look distinctive, and support business goals.

Website design and development services include responsive web design, website development, landing pages, portfolio websites, corporate websites, business websites, ecommerce website design, online stores, product pages, website redesign, user interface design, user experience design, front-end development, interactive elements, mobile-friendly layouts, website performance, accessibility, and search engine optimisation. Whether you need a website to introduce your services or an ecommerce platform to sell products online, the focus is on creating an experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Graphic design services help brands build a consistent and visual identity. Projects can include logo design, brand identity, visual branding, business cards, social media designs, promotional banners, digital adverts, flyers, posters, brochures, product graphics, presentation design, and creative marketing materials. Every design is guided by the brand’s message, audience, and visual direction, with attention to typography, colour, composition, clarity, and detail.

For ecommerce businesses, the goal is to make browsing, product discovery, and checkout straightforward. Online store experiences can include clear navigation, product catalogues, product descriptions, category pages, shopping carts, payment integration, and conversion-focused layouts. The result is a storefront designed to help customers understand products and move through the buying journey.

Working with clients worldwide, David Wix combines creative direction, practical development, and a detail-focused approach to deliver digital solutions that are attractive, useful, and built to grow. Whether you are launching a brand, improving an existing website, building an online store, or refreshing your visual identity, the aim is to create a digital presence that helps your business move forward.
